# SEO + UI Growth Roadmap

This file is the working checklist for growing Budget Holidays Hub without creating thin, orphaned, or hard-to-monetise pages.

## July 2026 Direction

The primary growth cluster is now inbound UK travel under `/visit-uk/`.
The existing `/guides/` library remains live as the secondary "Holidays from
the UK" cluster.

For inbound UK pages:

- Publish one field guide only when it answers a distinct route or cost
  question.
- Label every page as locally researched, personally visited or field tested.
- Link changing rules, fares and closures to the responsible official source.
- Record visit dates and real costs without exposing personal payment details.
- Prefer one original trip report over a batch of generic city pages.

## Current Priorities

1. Earn search demand for the `/visit-uk/` hub, Gatwick guide and London cornerstone.
2. Complete and publish one genuinely field-tested UK route with original images.
3. Refresh legacy travel pages in Search Console opportunity order.
4. Consolidate overlapping destination pages when two URLs answer the same decision.
5. Preserve fast, readable pages before adding heavier ad or affiliate units.

## Authority Refocus: 28 July 2026

The income, recipe and associated video libraries remain accessible to existing
visitors but are now `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap. They do not support
the site's primary travel purpose and recorded negligible organic visibility in
the preceding 90 days.

The outbound travel library remains indexable, but every legacy article now
discloses that prices are planning estimates until the page receives a
substantive evidence review. Unsupported `FAQPage` markup has been removed.

Initial Search Console refresh order:

1. `greece-vs-turkey-all-inclusive` — closest to page one.
2. `cheap-holidays-mauritius-from-uk` — highest impressions.
3. `best-travel-booking-websites-uk` — second-highest impressions.
4. `cheap-family-holidays-spain` — useful family intent.
5. `cheap-holidays-prague-from-uk` — emerging city-break demand.

A substantive refresh must replace unsupported fixed prices, reduce repeated
search-targeted copy, add visible official sources and change the review date
only after those checks are complete.

## Publishing Rules

- Every public page must have one H1, a unique title, a unique meta description, a canonical URL, and a clear next step.
- Every guide should answer the query quickly, then support the answer with realistic costs, timing, and booking decisions.
- Archive pages must remain `noindex` unless they are deliberately brought back
  into the site's primary travel purpose and pass a substantive evidence review.
- Avoid arbitrary word-count expansion. Add content only when it improves the reader's decision.
- Do not publish a page unless it has at least two internal links pointing to it from relevant existing pages.

## Internal Linking Rules

- Link from hubs to every important supporting page.
- Link from supporting pages back to the hub and to one next-step guide.
- Use natural anchors that describe the destination page.
- Prefer contextual paragraph links over standalone link blocks when adding links to existing guides.
- Recheck orphan pages after every content batch.

## Video Rules

- Use video pages when the video is the primary purpose of the page.
- Add `VideoObject` schema only to video-first pages.
- Validate every YouTube ID with oEmbed before publishing or reusing the embed.
- Keep videos visible and easy to access; do not hide them behind tabs.

## Schema Rules

- Add `Article` schema to article-style money pages and travel guides.
- Add `BreadcrumbList` schema when the page has a visible breadcrumb.
- Do not add `FAQPage` schema to ordinary editorial travel pages. Use a visible
  question-and-answer section without special markup when it genuinely helps.
- Do not duplicate or reuse schema blindly across pages.

## Monetization Readiness

- Keep affiliate and ad placements secondary until traffic grows.
- Reserve stable ad slots so future AdSense units do not cause layout shift.
- Keep affiliate CTAs relevant to the page intent and clearly separated from editorial advice.
- Maintain disclosure links in the global footer and near commercial CTAs when needed.

## Post-Publish QA

- Confirm the page appears in `sitemap.xml` or is intentionally `noindex`.
- Confirm all internal links resolve to real files.
- Confirm images include alt text.
- Confirm schema parses as valid JSON-LD.
- Confirm mobile and desktop layouts remain readable.
- Confirm at least two relevant internal pages link to every new page.
