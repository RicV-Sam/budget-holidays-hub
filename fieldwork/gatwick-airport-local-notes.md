# Gatwick Airport local notes

## Evidence status

- Source: owner-provided first-hand local perspective.
- Captured: 28 July 2026.
- Public label: `Local perspective · personally used`.
- Not labelled `field tested`: no dated end-to-end journey, measured timings,
  receipts or accessibility checks were supplied for this page.

## First-hand observations

- Gatwick is one of the owner's favourite UK airports.
- It feels straightforward for a London airport and has an easy rail route
  into central London.
- North Terminal is the owner's preferred terminal and feels bigger or more
  open to them.
- South Terminal feels smaller or more compact to them.
- The owner associates North Terminal with easyJet.
- The terminals are easy to transfer between.
- The direct railway connection from South Terminal is a major advantage.

## Operational facts verified on 28 July 2026

- Gatwick Airport lists easyJet at North Terminal.
- The railway station is connected to South Terminal.
- Gatwick calls the terminal link its free inter-terminal shuttle, not a
  mainline train.
- The shuttle runs every few minutes, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with a
  journey time of about two minutes.
- Gatwick Express and Southern serve London Victoria.
- Thameslink serves London Bridge, Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon
  and St Pancras International.
