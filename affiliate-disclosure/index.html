Affiliate Disclosure

How We Support Our Research

Budget Holidays Hub is an independent UK travel research site. To provide our guides for free and maintain our detailed research into budget travel trends, we sometimes earn a commission when you click on links and make a booking.

How It Works

When you click on a marked link in our guides to a booking platform or another travel-related service and complete a qualifying action, we may receive a commission from the provider. This can include a fixed fee when an eligible customer starts a free trial, such as an Audible trial promoted for travel listening. The provider pays this commission; it does not add a charge to your transaction.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Our Amazon links are marked as affiliate links close to the point where you click. We may earn a commission if you purchase through one of those links, at no extra cost to you. Amazon controls its own prices, sellers and availability, so always verify the current details on Amazon before buying.

It Costs You Nothing Extra

The commission we earn has no impact on the price you pay. Free trials may automatically renew as paid memberships unless cancelled before the renewal date. We state that close to the relevant link and encourage you to check the current price, eligibility rules and cancellation terms on the provider’s site before confirming.

Maintaining Editorial Independence

Our editorial standards are strictly separated from our commercial relationships. We only link to providers that our research shows offer genuine value and reliable service to UK travellers. A commission payment never influences our "Best Value" rankings or the objectivity of our destination reviews.

Our Commitment to You

Our priority is always the traveller. We are committed to transparency and to providing the most accurate, useful, and unbiased budget travel advice available to UK holidaymakers.

Thank you for supporting Budget Holidays Hub by using our affiliate links, which allows us to keep our expert research and guides accessible to everyone.
