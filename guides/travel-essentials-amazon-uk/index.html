Worth it when

Digital luggage scale

Your packed bag is close to the airline allowance, or you expect to bring purchases home. A quick check can prevent repacking at the desk or an excess-baggage charge.

Cabin bag only

Power bank

Useful for long transfers, digital boarding passes and map-heavy days. From 27 March 2026, UK CAA passenger guidance says power banks must be in carry-on baggage, individually protected when not in use and limited to two per person.

Check
Visible watt-hour rating, airline rules and the correct cable. Up to 100Wh is allowed without operator approval under the CAA rules.
On board
Do not recharge the power bank; the CAA says it should not be used to charge another device in flight.

03

Before checkout

Four things not to buy automatically

The cheapest travel item is the one already in your cupboard.

  • A bulky travel pillowfor a short daytime flight where it becomes another item to carry.
  • An RFID walletunless its size, organisation or security features solve a separate need.
  • Oversized toiletrieswhen small refillable containers or buying at the destination is simpler.
  • A voltage converterunless the appliance label and destination voltage show that you genuinely need one.
04

Final cabin-bag check

The product does not override the rule

Bag size and weightAirlines set their own allowances. Check the exact fare and operating carrier before leaving home.

LiquidsRules depend on each airport in your itinerary. Do not assume the outbound rule applies on the return.

Lithium batteriesPower banks belong in carry-on baggage and must meet current airline and CAA requirements.

Travel essentials FAQ

Are these specific products personally tested?

No. This is a category-level checklist, not a product review. The Amazon links open search results so you can compare current options, reviews, dimensions, sellers and prices. We do not claim hands-on testing where none occurred.

Why are there no prices?

Amazon prices, sellers and availability can change quickly. Showing a static price could mislead you, so the current price is left for you to verify on Amazon before purchasing.

Do I need all eight items?

No. Use the journey sorter and the “skip” note under each item. A typical traveller may only need one or two—and may already own suitable alternatives.
